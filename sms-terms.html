SMS messaging program operated by Sentinel Alpha LLC, doing business as Covered Down

About this program

Covered Down uses SMS text messaging to respond to business owners who ask about our services. Covered Down is an assumed name (DBA) of Sentinel Alpha LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company based in Murfreesboro. Messages come from (629) 288-8756.

How you opt in

You opt in one of two ways: by checking the consent box when you submit the contact form on this website, or by texting (629) 288-8756 yourself. We do not text anyone who has not done one of those two things. The consent box is optional, and you can submit the form without it.

What we send

Replies to your inquiry, questions needed to scope your request, and scheduling messages for a call. We do not send marketing blasts.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to make a purchase or receive services.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below.

Privacy

We do not sell your mobile number or your consent to receive text messages, and we do not share them with third parties for their marketing. See the Privacy Policy for how message data is handled.
