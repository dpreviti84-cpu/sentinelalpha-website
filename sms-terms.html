76 Fence South Nashville - SMS Program Terms

SMS messaging program operated by Sentinel Alpha LLC on behalf of 76 Fence South Nashville

About this program

76 Fence South Nashville uses SMS text messaging to respond to customer inquiries about fencing services in the Murfreesboro area. This program is operated on 76 Fence South Nashville's behalf by Sentinel Alpha LLC, a Tennessee-based technology provider.

How you opt in

You opt in to this program by texting +1 (615) 908-0810 directly. Initiating contact by text constitutes your express consent to receive SMS responses from this number. No outbound or unsolicited messages are sent to any number that has not first texted in. All messages are sent in direct response to customer-initiated contact.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your conversation with us.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Privacy

This program is operated by Sentinel Alpha LLC. See the Sentinel Alpha Privacy Policy for information on how message data is handled. For information about 76 Fence South Nashville's business practices, see their Privacy Policy.
