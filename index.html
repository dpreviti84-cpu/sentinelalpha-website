The Problem

Small businesses get sold software. Nobody installs it.

A login, a template, a monthly charge, and a support queue. The owner is left to wire the website to the CRM, the CRM to the phone, the phone to the calendar, and the calendar to the invoice. Most never finish. The lead that texts at 7pm on a Friday goes unanswered, and the one that does get a reply never lands in a record anyone can find. That is not a software problem. It is an installation problem, and it is the one we fix.
